2. Valuation (real DCF)

The model value is far from the market price (-915.2%). This usually means an input does not reflect the company's economics: after IAS 29 the operating margin can look low because of revalued depreciation, and a monetary gain from negative working capital can be an economic return that is not in operating profit. A reverse DCF (the margin or growth the market prices in) is then a better starting point. Not an investment view.
